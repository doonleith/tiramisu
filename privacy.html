Privacy

Your money stays yours.

Tiramisu is a personal finance tracker. This page explains, in plain English, what information the app uses and why.

Tiramisu is currently a personal beta. This is a clear product summary, not a substitute for a formal privacy notice before a wider public launch.

What we store

When you use Tiramisu, we store the information needed to provide the app: your sign-in identity from Google, your money spaces, transactions, categories, notes and recurring-payment settings. If you share a money space, its members can see the entries in that space.

How we use it

Your information is used only to run Tiramisu: to sign you in, show your entries, calculate totals, and support the features you choose. Tiramisu does not connect to your bank accounts or collect bank credentials; entries are manual.

Misu

If you choose to chat with Misu, your message and the relevant information needed to answer it may be processed by the AI service that powers Misu. Misu prepares changes for your review; it does not save a transaction without your confirmation.

Where it is kept

Sign-in and app data are stored using Supabase. Google handles Google sign-in. The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We use these providers only to operate the service.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. You control what you add and who you invite into a shared money space. For help with data in a shared space, contact the person who created or invited you to that space.
