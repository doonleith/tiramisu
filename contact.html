Contact & feedback

Help make Tiramisu clearer.

Tiramisu is currently shared by invitation while it is being developed. Feedback from early users is especially useful.

Need help with a shared space?

Please contact the person who invited you. They can help with access, explain how the space is organised, or pass feedback on to the Tiramisu team.

Found something that feels wrong?

Do not rely on a figure you think may be incorrect. Check the underlying entries first, then let the space owner know what you saw, which money space it was in, and the date it happened.

Before a public launch

A public support contact and formal privacy contact will be added before Tiramisu is opened beyond invited users.
