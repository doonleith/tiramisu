Terms

Simple terms for a simple tool.

Tiramisu helps you record and understand the figures you enter. By using it, you agree to use it responsibly and keep your sign-in secure.

What Tiramisu does

Tiramisu is a manual tracking tool. It shows calculations based on the entries in your money spaces. You are responsible for checking that those entries are accurate and for deciding how to use the information.

Not financial advice

Tiramisu and Misu provide organisation, summaries and arithmetic. They do not provide financial, investment, tax, credit, insurance or legal advice, and should not be treated as a recommendation or decision-making service.

Shared spaces

Only invite people you trust. People who join a shared money space can view its entries and add or edit information according to the app’s available features. The space owner is responsible for managing access.

Availability

Tiramisu is provided as a personal beta and may change, pause or be unavailable from time to time. Keep your own records for anything important.

Updates

These terms may be updated as Tiramisu develops. The latest version will always appear on this page.
