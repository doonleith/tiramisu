Security & your data

Private by design.

Tiramisu is designed to keep your financial entries separated by person and money space.

Sign-in

You sign in through Google. Tiramisu does not see or store your Google password.

Private money spaces

Your data is protected by database access rules, so you can access only your own spaces and any spaces you have been invited to join. A shared-space invite is a one-time link that expires after seven days.

Manual entries, not bank access

Tiramisu does not connect to bank accounts and never asks for bank credentials. The income and expenses you see are the entries recorded in the app.

Control and care

Keep your Google account secure and only share a money space with people you trust. If you see a person in a space who should not have access, the space owner can remove them.

Misu conversations

Misu only prepares a transaction after a conversation. You always review and confirm a proposed addition or change before it is saved.
